# Third-party references

The MDL, VVD, and VTX record layouts follow the public Source SDK 2013 headers, in particular `public/studio.h` and `public/optimize.h`

[SourcePP](https://github.com/craftablescience/sourcepp), in particular its `mdlpp` implementation, was used as a development reference and as a differential test oracle. SourcePP and its test assets are not linked, downloaded, or redistributed by this crate.
